## Eliza Chen (陈仪真)

**MA in Sociology at Minzu University of China** | Organizational & Digital Sociologist

I'm a sociologist exploring how digital technologies reshape social organization and relationships. My fieldwork examines emerging organizational forms, especially how decentralized systems transform trust-building, skill-sharing, and community formation in rural contexts.

My research sits at the intersection of **organizational sociology** and **the sociology of technology**. I am interested in how technology, organizations, and social relationships co-evolve.
